# Portuguese verb morphology

- Source: **UniMorph Portuguese (por)**, https://github.com/unimorph/por , downloaded from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/unimorph/por/master/por on 2026-10-28. Upstream README attributes its source to Wikipedia and specifies **CC BY-SA 3.0**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/ . The original README is preserved in `sources/unimorph-README.md`, the entire original data snapshot in `sources/unimorph-por.tsv.gz`, and its SHA-256 in `sources/unimorph-checksum.json`. The license text is in `licenses/CC-BY-SA-3.0.txt`.
- `../verb-data.js` is an adaptation: verb rows only, deduplicated into indexed feature tables, containing 4,001 lemmas and 303,996 original verb-form rows. The dictionary adaptation remains available under **CC BY-SA 3.0**, with attribution to the UniMorph Portuguese contributors and the source they cite. No endorsement is implied.
- **Changes made for this app**: `../tools/build_verbs.py` adds Brazilian first-person-plural preterite spellings ending in `-amos` alongside upstream European Portuguese `-ámos`, only for the specific `V;1;PL;IND;PST;PFV` feature. 3,334 such alternatives are generated. Original spellings are preserved. The complete build script and source allow offline reproduction: `python3 tools/build_verbs.py`.
- `../verbs.js` supplies original Chinese labels and Brazilian pronoun-use notes for these features. The upstream data is Portuguese-wide, not a dedicated Brazilian dataset; vós/tu and European variants are retained and labeled. Shared forms are deliberately listed with **all** matching lemma/feature analyses. Without syntactic context a word's exact part of speech, person and tense cannot be determined. For example `foi` matches both ser and ir; `trabalhamos` may be present or preterite in Brazil; noun/verb homographs can occur.
- This dataset lists simple inflections. It does not analyze multiword compound tenses (e.g. tenho falado), clitics or entire sentences, and it does not cover every Portuguese verb. Failed lookup does not prove that a word is not a verb. Definition data and morphology data are separate datasets with their own licenses.

Retain this attribution, the license, source snapshot and build script when redistributing the verb dataset or adaptations.
